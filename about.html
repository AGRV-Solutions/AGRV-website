Our Story

Scientists who got tired of watching organizations overpay for software that didn't fit

AGRV Solutions was founded on a simple premise: specialized organizations deserve software and data solutions built precisely for the way they actually work — not forced into a generic SaaS product designed for everyone and perfect for no one.

Why We Exist

Built by researchers, for organizations that take their data seriously

Our founders — a PhD research scientist and an MSc data engineer — spent years working inside research institutions and data-driven organizations. They saw the same problem repeat itself: teams running critical workflows on spreadsheets, outdated tools, or subscription platforms that held their data hostage. Switching costs were high. Vendor lock-in was real. Privacy was an afterthought.

AGRV Solutions was created to offer an alternative. We build software, data systems, and AI pipelines that are designed exactly for your environment, delivered with complete ownership, and hosted where you want — on your servers, your network, or your hardware. We don't make money when you use the software. We make money when we build it well and you trust us with the next project.

That alignment of incentives is at the core of everything we do.

Our Process

How every engagement works

We don't start writing code until we fully understand your problem. Every project follows the same structured approach.



01

Discovery & Scoping

Free consultation to understand your workflows, constraints, data environment, and goals. We document requirements and identify risks before any cost is incurred.

02

Proposal & Architecture

A detailed technical proposal with cost estimate, timeline, and architecture diagram. You review and approve before a single line of code is written.

03

Build & Iterate

Milestone-based delivery with regular check-ins. You see working software early and often — not just at the finish line. Scope changes are handled transparently.

04

Delivery & Handoff

Full source code, documentation, deployment guides, and a walkthrough session. We train your team to operate and maintain the system — no dependency created.
